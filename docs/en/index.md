## Architecture authority and offline sources

CIRNET Rules owns the [canonical architecture and source policy](https://github.com/cirnetlab/cirnet-rules/blob/master/en/policies/architecture.md).
For offline engineering and verification, read the relevant files from an approved local Rules checkout or a pinned artifact with verified provenance.

The [archived provenance table](https://github.com/cirnetlab/cirnet-remote/blob/1921cbb54bf95a48a6bcc81ad7256c67559947e2/docs/en/index.md#architecture-snapshot-provenance) records the retired Remote copies and their original digests.
